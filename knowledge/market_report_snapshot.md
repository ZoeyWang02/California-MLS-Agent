## Reading the Numbers

- **San Jose and Oakland are selling above list** (list-to-close over 100%), a sign of strong buyer demand relative to inventory in the Bay Area cities in this snapshot.
- **Palm Desert has the longest average days on market (70.4)** and the widest gap between average and median price of the cities listed, suggesting a slower-moving market with a small number of high-price outliers pulling the average up.
- **Median price is consistently lower than average price in every city** — expected right-skew in home price distributions, where a smaller number of very high-priced sales pull the average above the typical (median) transaction.
- **San Diego has the highest sold volume (4,158)** among cities in this snapshot, over the trailing 12 months.

This snapshot should be treated as a point-in-time reference tied to this project's local dataset, not a live or complete market report.
